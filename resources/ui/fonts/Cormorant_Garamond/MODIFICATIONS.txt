CadenzaSerif-Regular.ttf and CadenzaSerif-Italic.ttf are Cormorant Garamond
Regular and Italic (Copyright 2015 the Cormorant Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1,
no Reserved Font Name) with one change: the `name` table declares the family as
"Cadenza Serif".

Nothing else was touched — not an outline, not a metric, not a kerning pair.
The change was made by scripts/rename_font.py and exists so that the font
shipped inside Cadenza is the font Cadenza draws with, on a machine that has
Cormorant Garamond installed as much as on one that does not.

The originals are at https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond.
